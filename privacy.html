Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Ry's Rad Waves is made by Braickish LLC. We keep as little about you as the game needs, we don't sell it, and there are no ads or trackers.

Without an account

You can surf without signing in. Your browser keeps your best score, the last wave you picked, your last ride (for replays) and your builder design in its own local storage. That stays on your device: we never see it.

With an account

  • Your email, to send your sign-in links. It's never shown to other players.
  • Your profile: handle, name and jersey. These are public, on your page and the board.
  • Breaks you save, with who can see each one (private, unlisted or public).
  • Your plan: whether you have the Wave Pass or Pro, and until when.

Contest results and ride replays will join this list when contests arrive, and the public ones will show on your page.

Payments

Stripe and its Link handle payments (see the terms). We never see your card. Stripe tells us what you bought, when, and whether it's paid, and we keep your Stripe customer number to open your billing page. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what it holds.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts the accounts database and sends the sign-in emails.
  • Stripe takes payments.
  • GitHub Pages serves the game's pages.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and some code libraries from public CDNs (esm.sh, jsDelivr). Like any website, these see your IP address when your browser fetches from them.

Your choices

Change your handle, name or email on the account page. Delete your account there too: that deletes your profile, breaks and plan from our database for good (Stripe keeps its payment records, as the law requires).

Questions, or want a copy of what we hold? Write to howdy@rysradwaves.com.
